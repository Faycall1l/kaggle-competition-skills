# The organizer's onset selector guarantees the queue lands ONLY on T+30 - a window generator must enforce it
Date: 2026-10-22
Competition: 2026-ieee-big-data-traffic-flow-bench
Finding: The scoring spec says an onset window has "no queue visible in the history and a queue in the horizon", but the selector keeps the EARLIEST qualifying origin, so for a queue episode starting at tau the qualifying origins are tau-30 .. tau-5 and the earliest is tau-30 - the queue therefore first appears at T+30, by construction. Measured on all 5 released train onset windows: queued cells per horizon step = 0;0;0;0;0;N (N = 2..3). A naive generator that accepts any origin with "no queue in the history + a queue somewhere in the horizon" produces windows where the queue lights up at T+25 as well, which makes the champion's `--onset-rule` (zero out T+5..T+25, predict only the final step) look far worse than it is. The same reasoning applies to the ongoing condition: the queue must still be running at the first forecast step. Enforcing both rules (onset: steps 0..4 empty and step 5 non-empty; ongoing: a queue at T+5 and at T+30) makes the generated distribution match the released windows (onset mean queued cells per step {0,0,0,0,0,2.5}; ongoing {26.5,28.1,27.3,27.0,27.0,27.5}).
Evidence: released train onset windows hz_pos_by_step = "0;0;0;0;0;3", "0;0;0;0;0;2", "0;0;0;0;0;3", "0;0;0;0;0;2", "0;0;0;0;0;3"; all 5 released ongoing windows have a queue in every one of the 6 horizon steps; generator v2 (fixed) yields 0 windows with a queue before the final step, v1 yielded 24/24
Applies when: rebuilding a window-based metric - the window distribution is a property of the SELECTOR, not just of the stated condition, and a mismatch silently reweights the metric
